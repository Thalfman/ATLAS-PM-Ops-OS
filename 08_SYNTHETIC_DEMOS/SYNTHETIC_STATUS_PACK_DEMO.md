# SYNTHETIC_STATUS_PACK_DEMO.md

## Purpose

This synthetic demo shows how ATLAS can support weekly status reporting, action tracking, risk/issue clarity, and executive narrative drafting without using any employer data.

Everything below is fictional. It does not represent Motorola Solutions, any real program, any real customer, any real contract, or any internal schedule.

## AI-assisted executive summary draft

Project Northstar Demo is progressing with the initial weekly status template completed on schedule and the action tracker pilot delayed by two days due to owner availability. The primary PM/Ops risk is not technical execution; it is operating-rhythm discipline, specifically unclear action ownership, missing due dates, and inconsistent decision capture. The recommended next step is to confirm owners and due dates for all open actions, then use the upcoming risk review workshop to validate whether the simplified reporting format is practical for recurring use.

## AI-assisted variance narrative draft

The action tracker pilot moved from 2026-05-15 to 2026-05-17, creating a two-day variance. The known driver is owner availability for confirming action assignments. The impact is limited to the timing of pilot feedback and may also shift the lessons learned draft by two days. Corrective action is to confirm owners and due dates before the next meeting closes and to maintain a visible action aging view for follow-up.

## Human review checklist

Before using this pattern with approved employer data, confirm:

- The AI tool is approved for the data.
- The data type is approved for that tool.
- Program/customer/contract/schedule/financial/technical details are allowed in that workflow.
- A human validates all facts, dates, owners, and variance causes.
- The final output is stored only in approved systems.

## Demo value

This demo illustrates a safe, practical AI-enabled workflow:

1. Structure raw PM/Ops inputs.
2. Flag missing owner/date/decision details.
3. Draft a concise executive narrative.
4. Draft a variance explanation.
5. Preserve human review and approval.
