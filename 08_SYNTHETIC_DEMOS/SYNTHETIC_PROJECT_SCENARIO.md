## Purpose

A single coherent fictional PM/Ops scenario that every demo in `08_SYNTHETIC_DEMOS/` draws from. Demos that share a backdrop are easier to walk through end-to-end with a manager or peer because the cast of characters, milestones, risks, and issues persist across reports. The scenario is deliberately generic and process-focused; it never resembles federal, defense, RF, or any real Motorola Solutions program.

## Synthetic data rules (binding for every demo)

1. Every demo file opens with a bold synthetic label that names the fiction.
2. All names, dates, milestones, owners, risks, issues, and numbers are obvious fictions. No real program names, customer names, contract names.
3. Financials, when present, are illustrative round numbers (e.g., 100, 1000) with no currency commitment; they look obviously synthetic.
4. Demos walk **input → AI step → human review → output** as a pattern. They are not finished products and not deployable artifacts.
5. Demos never blend in real data on the fly to "make it more realistic." That defeats the purpose of the pack.
6. Demos never claim deployment-readiness. Cross-references to W-NN / P-NN cards make the migration path explicit; nothing implies the demo itself is approved.
7. If a future review request would loosen any of the rules above, surface as a posture issue and log a new decision rather than silently relaxing the rule.
