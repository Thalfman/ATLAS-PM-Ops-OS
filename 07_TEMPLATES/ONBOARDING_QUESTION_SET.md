## Purpose

A safe question bank for managers, peers, IT, security, compliance, and program contacts during onboarding. Mirrors the discovery script (`07_TEMPLATES/FIRST_WEEK_DISCOVERY_SCRIPT.md`) but splits questions by audience and marks which questions are appropriate during clearance-limited periods vs after clearance. Tom tailors the set before each conversation; not every question fits every meeting.

## Governance envelope

- **Data category** (per `06_GOVERNANCE/DATA_SENSITIVITY_DECISION_MODEL.md`, "Data categories"): Public-or-synthetic. The question bank is generic; answers Tom captures are Tom-personal and stay in personal preparation notes unless generic enough for ATLAS.
- **Tool environment** (same file, "Tool environments"): ATLAS-local Markdown for the bank itself; Tom-personal notes for tailored copies used in real conversations.
- **Review intensity** (per `06_GOVERNANCE/HUMAN_REVIEW_AND_AUDITABILITY_MODEL.md`, "Three review intensities"): Light. Tom self-reviews before each conversation.
- **Per-domain review pattern** (same file, "Per-domain review patterns"): Status and reporting outputs (when applied to summarizing answers).

## Use posture

- Listen first; ask second.
- Tailor the question list before each meeting; do not bring 20 questions when 4 would do.
- Some questions are appropriate only after a working relationship is established; those are marked **[after rapport]** below.
- Some questions require an approval context (manager 1:1, IT or security office hours, compliance intake); those are marked **[venue-specific]**.
- Some questions are best held until after clearance lands; those are marked **[post-clearance]**.
- If the venue is unclear, default to "not now."
- Capture answers in personal preparation notes only; do not paraphrase restricted content.

## Question banks by audience

### A. Manager (1:1 or small team)

Role and success criteria

1. What would make my first 30, 60, and 90 days successful from your perspective?
2. Which PM/Ops responsibilities are highest priority for me to take on first?
3. Where do you see the most reporting, schedule, finance, or action-tracking friction today?
4. What should I avoid changing in my first months until I understand the environment better?
5. Which operating rhythms (meetings, cadences, reviews) should I observe first?

Working style and feedback

6. What is your preferred cadence for our 1:1s, and how do you like status framed (verbal, written, dashboard)?
7. How do you prefer I escalate when I see a risk or issue I cannot resolve at my level?
8. What does "good" look like for a weekly status update or a variance narrative in this environment?
9. Are there reviewers or stakeholders I should loop in before publishing anything externally?
10. When you have given feedback to PMs that "stuck," what made the feedback land?

Clearance-limited contribution **[venue-specific]**

11. While clearance is pending, what work would be most useful for me to do?
12. Are there observable rhythms I can sit in on that do not require clearance?
13. Are there generic process maps, templates, or training material you would like me to study or refine?
14. Are there public PMI / EVM / project-controls topics where you would like me to deepen and then brief back?
15. Is there appetite for synthetic demos of AI-assisted PM/Ops workflows, framed as personal preparation?

Tom's positioning

16. How would you like me to frame what I bring on AI integration in conversations with peers and stakeholders?
17. Are there topics where I should be careful not to lead - either because they are owned elsewhere or because they are sensitive?
18. How visible would you like my early work to be to leadership above you?

### B. Peers (project controls, PM, scheduler, EVM analyst, finance analyst)

Working relationship

1. What does a good handoff look like between your role and the PM role?
2. Which interfaces between our work cause the most friction today?
3. What would make my arrival the most useful for you in the next month?
4. Are there reports or reconciliations you do that I should learn before I touch any of the inputs?
5. Where is the documentation I should read first to come up to speed responsibly?

Tooling and process **[after rapport]**

6. Which tools and views do you use most in a normal week?
7. Are there tool quirks or workarounds I should know about so I do not break them by accident?
8. Where do you wish PM oversight added value, and where does it create overhead?
9. Are there places where AI-assisted drafting or reconciliation could help, if approved and reviewed responsibly?
10. Who should I shadow or sit with for the first couple of weeks to learn how the work actually flows?

### C. IT

Approved tools and approved data **[venue-specific]**

1. Which AI tools are approved for employee use today, and for what data categories?
2. Is there an internal page or document that lists the current approved AI tools?
3. Are there AI tools explicitly disallowed even for personal-style use on a work device?
4. How are new AI tools added to the approved list, and roughly how long does that take?
5. Are there approved AI capabilities (summarization, drafting, translation) where the tool itself is constrained, regardless of which product provides them?

Account and access **[venue-specific]**

6. What accounts and access requests should I have open right now, and what is the typical turnaround?
7. Are there onboarding modules I must complete before specific accesses are granted?
8. Are there local-machine policies I should know (peripherals, removable media, browser extensions) so I do not trigger a violation by accident?

Cross-references to `06_GOVERNANCE/EMPLOYER_TOOL_APPROVAL_QUESTION_SET.md` Sections A-J apply when the conversation is the right venue for them.

### D. Security

Restricted data categories **[venue-specific]**

1. What data categories are off-limits to AI tools regardless of approval status?
2. Where is the line between "internal but unrestricted" and "internal and restricted" for AI input?
3. How should program names, customer names, and contract identifiers be handled in AI inputs?
4. What would constitute a reportable event if AI use went wrong?
5. Are there specific labeling or handling requirements I should learn before I touch any internal content?

Clearance pipeline **[venue-specific, post-clearance discussion]**

6. What is the expected sequence of accesses once clearance lands?
7. Are there briefings or training I should request proactively?
8. Are there areas where it would help to know what I am not allowed to see, so I do not stumble into them?

### E. Compliance and audit

Records, retention, and labeling **[venue-specific]**

1. Are AI outputs treated as records once they are used in official work? If so, where should they be stored?
2. Are there retention timelines specific to AI prompts and outputs, distinct from the underlying source data?
3. Should AI-assisted documents be labeled or watermarked in any way?
4. Are there reviewers or auditors who should be looped in for specific kinds of AI-assisted output?
5. Are there parts of the reporting cycle where the audit envelope is especially tight?

### F. Program leadership **[post-rapport, post-clearance, venue-specific]**

1. Which areas of the program reporting cycle would you most want improved this fiscal year?
2. Where does reporting quality have the most leverage on leadership decisions?
3. Are there places where AI-assisted drafting is in scope, with the right approvals and review?
4. What would be a failure mode worth me knowing about so I do not repeat it?
5. How would you like me to surface a risk or issue that I see but cannot characterize fully?

## Tailoring discipline

Before any real conversation, Tom:

1. Picks 3-5 questions from the relevant audience set.
2. Drops anything not appropriate for the venue, the level of rapport, or the clearance state.
3. Saves the tailored list to personal preparation notes - not back into the master ATLAS file.
4. After the conversation, captures generic-enough answers in `07_TEMPLATES/LISTENING_PLAN.md` daily entry; restricted detail stays out of ATLAS.
